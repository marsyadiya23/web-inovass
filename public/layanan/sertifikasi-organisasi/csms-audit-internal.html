Pengertian Jasa Audit Internal CSMS

Jasa Audit Internal CSMS adalah layanan profesional yang membantu perusahaan melakukan independent assessment terhadap implementasi Contractor Safety Management System, verifikasi compliance, identifikasi non-conformance, dan memberikan recommendations untuk continuous improvement contractor safety management.

Manfaat Audit Internal CSMS

Independent Assessment

Penilaian objektif dan independen terhadap CSMS implementation

Compliance Verification

Verifikasi kepatuhan CSMS terhadap regulatory dan internal standards

Non-Conformance Detection

Identifikasi non-conformance dan gap dalam contractor management

Risk Identification

Identifikasi emerging risk dari contractor operations

Management Input

Input untuk management review dan strategic decision making

Continuous Improvement

Drive continuous improvement melalui audit findings dan recommendations

Proses Audit Internal CSMS

1

Audit Planning

Menyusun audit plan, scope definition, audit criteria, dan preparation audit checklist berbasis CSMS requirements.

2

Document Review

Review dokumentasi CSMS, contractor records, incident reports, dan verification evidence compliance.

3

Field Audit

Conduct field audit, interview HSE team dan kontraktor, observasi contractor activities, dan evidence collection.

6

Follow-up Audit

Follow-up audit untuk verify effectiveness corrective action dan closure audit findings.

5

Corrective Action Plan

Develop corrective action plan untuk non-conformance, assign responsibility, dan set timeline untuk improvement.

4

Audit Report

Penyusunan audit report, findings presentation, recommendations, dan closing meeting dengan management.

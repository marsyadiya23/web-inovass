Pengertian Jasa Konsultasi Health & Safety Management

Jasa Konsultasi Health & Safety Management adalah layanan profesional yang membantu organisasi dalam merancang, mengimplementasikan, dan mengelola sistem manajemen kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja (K3) secara komprehensif untuk melindungi karyawan dari risiko kecelakaan kerja, penyakit akibat kerja, dan bahaya di tempat kerja, memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan K3, serta menciptakan budaya keselamatan yang kuat melalui pendekatan sistematis berbasis standar ISO 45001, PP 50/2012, dan best practices K3.

Manfaat Konsultasi Health & Safety Management

Occupational Safety System

Sistem keselamatan kerja terintegrasi: hazard identification, risk control, safety procedures, emergency response, dan accident prevention untuk zero accident

Occupational Health Program

Program kesehatan kerja: medical surveillance, health risk assessment, occupational hygiene, ergonomics, dan disease prevention untuk workforce wellness

Accident Reduction

Pengurangan kecelakaan kerja: incident investigation, root cause analysis, corrective action, safety improvement, dan behavior-based safety

Safety Culture Development

Pengembangan budaya K3: safety leadership, employee engagement, safety awareness, safety training, dan commitment building untuk safety excellence

K3 Regulatory Compliance

Kepatuhan regulasi K3: compliance audit, legal requirement identification, SMK3 PP 50/2012 compliance, dan regulatory reporting

ISO 45001 Implementation

Implementasi ISO 45001: OHSMS development, certification preparation, internal audit, management review, dan continuous improvement

Tahapan Jasa Konsultasi Health & Safety Management

1

Safety Assessment & Gap Analysis

Evaluasi kondisi K3 existing, identifikasi hazard dan risiko, assessment compliance terhadap regulasi K3, review safety procedures, dan gap analysis terhadap ISO 45001 dan PP 50/2012

2

HSM System Design

Perancangan sistem manajemen K3: kebijakan K3, sasaran K3, struktur organisasi K3, program kerja K3, dan emergency response plan sesuai kondisi organisasi

3

Safety Documentation

Penyusunan dokumen K3: safety manual, safety procedures, JSA, work permit, safety checklist, emergency plan, dan safety records sesuai standar

6

Safety Improvement Support

Pendampingan continuous improvement K3, monitoring safety performance, support compliance maintenance, dan assistance untuk safety culture development

5

Safety Audit & Review

Pelaksanaan internal audit K3, safety inspection, incident investigation, management review K3, dan verification readiness untuk sertifikasi SMK3 atau ISO 45001

4

Implementation & Training

Implementasi sistem K3, sosialisasi program K3, safety awareness training, emergency drill, P2K3 establishment, dan coaching untuk safety compliance
